Vice Coast is a GTA 6-style cover art maker that runs entirely offline in the browser. Upload your own photo (or use one of the two built-in procedural sample scenes), arrange it across 6 layouts, apply one of 6 colour grades, edit the title, subtitle and tagline with GTA-style typography, then export the finished cover as a PNG (1080×1080, 1080×1350 or 1920×1080). Your photo never leaves your machine.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080/ in your browser (Node 22, no dependencies, no build step). You can also open `app/index.html` directly.
